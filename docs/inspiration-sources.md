Prada's season and URL folder are not treated as an exact publication date. Source dates never change when the API responds or the user retries. Maintainers must open/review a source before changing its checked date. Recheck links and seasonal relevance during later maintenance; a checked source may subsequently move or become unavailable.

All card art is neutral vector iconography from the existing Lucide dependency. No source/retailer photographs were copied, hotlinked or generated to impersonate products. No shopping, affiliate or live-ranking action is provided. Source links open separately with `noopener noreferrer`.

Browser fixtures verify displayed dates, URLs and failure/retry behavior; they do not prove external publisher availability. Actual source inspection is a separate research check. The [feature inventory](feature-inventory.md) and [testing report](testing.md) describe that distinction.
